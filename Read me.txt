ALL ABOUT DEVIN

For a team getting started
Open the Team Rollout & Quick-Start Map on the contents page. Start with the
practice desk, choose the right way to run a task, then try the read-only
Exchange lab with the sample mailbox list.

On an iPad
Open the published book in Safari while connected. Wait for “Ready offline”.
You can then reopen the same address, search, and read without a connection.
To keep a shortcut, use Share and Add to Home Screen. Open the shortcut once
while connected and wait for its offline copy to finish.

On a computer
Unzip the download, open the all-about-devin folder, and double-click
“Read All About Devin.html”. The chapters, pictures, search and Exchange kit
are inside that one file. You can give the file or the complete ZIP to a
colleague. Live Devin sessions and official documentation need a connection.

The complete ZIP also contains the editable book. See UPDATING.md to rebuild
it with Python. The kit uses made-up mailbox data for practice.
